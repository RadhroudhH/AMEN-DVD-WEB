AMEN DVD: REFLECTION

AMEN DVD: REFLECTION is the physical release of my film "REFLECTION" and is the second edition of AMEN DVD (2025).

It's a project filled with passion and authenticity where I connect my experimental vision of independent filmmaking with the old World of technology that once was the prominent medium for media consumption.

Consisted of menus and easter eggs to find, this 2nd edition of AMEN DVD, like its predecessor, was the ground for me to play around how I can enrich the DVD experience and develop an immersive bond between the viewer and my films through this amazing platform.

In this modern age where digital subscriptions are your-go-to for multimedia infinite access, I truly believe that nothing will surpass the relief of really owning what you own not bound by any restrictions or blockages,
Dare I confirm that:
"Physical Media Is Not Dead."
